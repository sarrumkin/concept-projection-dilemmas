The four author branches each contributed 60 triplets. Initial auditor agreement with the authored relation was 154/240 and 221/240, with 140/240 joint agreement; after the masked letter/explanation correction, joint agreement was 225/240. The numeric recheck reproduces 111/240 for the embedding, 145/240 for Concept207, and 133/240 for Hybrid50, including the stored distances, bootstrap intervals, and random controls.

The archive also contains rejected authoring reports, initial audits, and the alignment attempt. These are retained to document the process and should not be read as final quality evidence. Some archival reports are in Russian; their language is explicitly preserved here as historical material. The concise English methods and data notes provide the public interpretation.
